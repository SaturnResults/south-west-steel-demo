Who we are

The South West's trusted partner in bespoke steel frame buildings.

Our own purpose-built factory sits right in the heart of Devon, equipped with cutting edge technology and staffed by our very own highly skilled team. We build for the agricultural, industrial and equestrian industries across the whole of the UK.

Our in-house service means that from the initial design to the grand reveal, we handle every stage of your project. Whether it's a barn to protect your livestock, a warehouse to support your growing business or a custom-built riding arena, you are in trusted hands.

How we build

From an empty field to the grand reveal.

Stage 01 · Survey

We come to you.

Our friendly experts visit your site, advise on the most affordable way to design your building, and check every measurement and level before anything is manufactured.

Stage 02 · Design

Modelled in 3D, in-house.

Our draughtsmen build a Tekla model of your building, then produce fabrication drawings designed to be easily understood on the shop floor, reducing any risk of error.

Stage 03 · Fabricate

Made in our Devon factory.

Certified coded fabricators turn those drawings into stanchions and rafters in our large, modern, purpose-built factory, five days a week.

Stage 04 · Deliver

On our own lorries.

Load lists are planned in the design office, then purlins, bracing and steelwork arrive on time with our own fleet of delivery vehicles.

Stage 05 · Complete

Built for today and tomorrow.

Roof, cladding and finishes complete a quality-assured building that works for you today, tomorrow and for years to come.
